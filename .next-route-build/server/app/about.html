About Bigfox

Who We Are

Founded in Chennai in 2016, Bigfox Engineering Pvt. Ltd. brings together industrial automation, security systems, component manufacturing and R&D. Our journey into connected mobility led to RoadLenz, combining our engineering experience with vehicle safety and fleet intelligence.

RoadLenz software helps customers track vehicles, view live locations, monitor telematics and access live video and two-way voice communication with compatible devices.

The RoadLenz website helps customers explore and purchase MDVR systems, GPS trackers, dashcams and related vehicle-safety products — supported by Bigfox's customer, installation and technical teams.
