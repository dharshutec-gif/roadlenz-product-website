What is Reports?

RoadLenz Reports organises recorded fleet information into structured views for operational review.

What is it used for?

Compare activity across vehicles and periods, investigate exceptions and support repeatable fleet performance discussions.

How it works

From fleet activity to useful reports.

  1. 01

    Collect

    Record available vehicle and device activity.

  2. 02

    Organise

    Structure data by vehicle, period and report type.

  3. 03

    Analyse

    Review the measures relevant to your operation.

  4. 04

    Export / Act

    Use available reports to support follow-up.
